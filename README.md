# RoNy's Store (Point of Sale System)

A robust and user-friendly Point of Sale (POS) system built with **Python**, designed to streamline retail operations. This application features an automated selling module, inventory maintenance with expiration tracking, and detailed financial reporting to ensure business efficiency and accuracy.

---

## 📱 Core Modules & Features

### 🧮 Sales & Computation Module
The primary interface for processing customer transactions with high precision and speed.

<img src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/511b0191-0600-4cb7-8385-d16eb4577f05" width="100%" alt="Sales Module Screenshot" />

* **Smart Category Filtering:** Dynamically organizes products by category (e.g., Drinks, Snacks) for faster item selection.
* **Real-Time Calculations:** Automatically computes Total Amount, Profit Margins, and Change Due to eliminate manual errors.
* **User-Friendly Interface:** Built for rapid data entry, ensuring short queues and efficient customer service.

---

### ⏰ Inventory & Expiration Management
Dedicated to maintaining product quality and ensuring store standards are met through proactive monitoring.

<img src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2ac670c7-58e3-4a1a-973e-0c0eadb1fee1" width="100%" alt="Maintenance Module Screenshot" />

* **Expiration Tracking:** Integrated alert system that identifies products approaching or past their expiration dates.
* **Data Management:** Fully functional module to create, view, update, and remove product records.
* **Stock Control:** Helps prevent the sale of outdated goods and keeps the digital inventory synced with physical stock.

---

### 📊 Business Analytics & Reports
Provides a high-level overview of business performance through data-driven insights.

<img src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/8ed80edd-bf02-432f-bf13-7f46fb039ee1" width="100%" alt="Sales Report Screenshot" />

* **Flexible Reporting:** Generate financial summaries based on custom timeframes—Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
* **Profit Analysis:** Monitors total revenue and net profit to help business owners analyze sales trends.
* **Performance Monitoring:** Visualizes business growth and identifies top-performing product categories.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
* **Programming Language:** Python 3.x
* **GUI Library:** [E.g., Tkinter / CustomTkinter / PyQt]
* **Data Handling:** Local File Storage (In-memory processing)
